RoomKey TV Remote — Support

Last updated: 2026-11-23

RoomKey TV Remote is an iPhone and iPad remote-control utility for compatible TVs on your local Wi-Fi network. On your Home Screen, build 1.0 (2) appears as Smart TV Remote.

Getting started

  1. Connect your iPhone or iPad and TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the TV, enable network/remote control:
    • Settings → Network & Internet → Home Network → IP Control (or, on older firmware, System → Network → Home Network Setup → IP Control).
    • Set Simple IP Control to On.
    • Set Authentication to Normal for PIN pairing (recommended), or Normal and Pre-Shared Key if you prefer PSK.
    • In Settings → Network, turn on Remote Start so the TV responds even when off.
  3. Open the app, tap Find TVs on Wi-Fi, choose your TV, then tap Get PIN from TV. The TV will display a 4-digit PIN — type it into the app to finish pairing.

Troubleshooting

The scanner can't find my TV
  • Confirm your device and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network or separate 5 GHz SSID).
  • Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and confirm it is enabled.
  • Wake the TV. The TV's network service often goes quiet when the screen is fully asleep on some firmware.
  • Use Enter IP Address Manually as a fallback. The TV's IP is visible under Settings → Network → Network status.
No PIN appears on the TV when I tap "Get PIN from TV"
  • The TV display must be on. The network service responds when the panel is asleep, but the PIN dialog can't render.
  • If the PIN does not appear, return to the TV’s Home screen and request a new PIN.
  • Confirm Authentication is set to Normal. If it's set to None, the API accepts any request without showing a PIN.
  • If the dialog flashed by too quickly, tap Show PIN Again in the app to re-trigger it.
The PIN was rejected
  • The PIN expires shortly after the dialog hides. Tap Show PIN Again for a fresh one.
  • Confirm you typed the digits exactly as shown on the TV.
  • If pairing keeps failing, switch the TV to Normal and Pre-Shared Key authentication, set a PSK on the TV, and enable Use Pre-Shared Key Instead in the app's Add TV screen.
Some commands work but others don't
  • Different TV models support different IRCC codes and app launch URIs. Available controls vary by TV model and firmware. Some app shortcuts may be unsupported on older models — open the TV's home screen manually and launch the app from there.
The widget doesn't control my TV
  • Open the app at least once and confirm a TV is paired and selected. The widget reads the selected TV from the shared App Group container.
  • Long-press the widget and choose Edit Widget to refresh its data.
How do I remove ads / save more than one TV?
  • In-app purchases are disabled in the current version. Multiple saved TVs are available without purchase.
I cannot find the upgrade option
  • In-app purchases are disabled in the current version while paid app account setup is completed.
  • Multiple saved TVs are available without purchase in this build.
How do I reset everything?
  • Open Settings → Reset All Settings in the app. This removes saved TVs, pairing credentials, and preferences from this device.

Contact

For help, open a GitHub issue with your TV model, iOS version, and a short description of what you tried. Issues are public. Keep pairing PINs, pre-shared keys, and other private information out of your report.
