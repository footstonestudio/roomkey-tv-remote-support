Privacy Policy

RoomKey TV Remote · Last updated: 2026-11-23

RoomKey TV Remote (the "App") is an independent iOS utility that lets you control compatible TVs over your local Wi-Fi network. This policy covers version 1.0, build 2.

What the App does on your device

  • Discovers compatible TVs on your local Wi-Fi network using Bonjour service browsing.
  • Sends remote-control commands (power, volume, input switching, text entry, app launching) directly from your iPhone or iPad to your TV over the local network.
  • Stores, locally on your device, the TV name, local IP address, and pairing credentials (PIN-derived auth cookie or Pre-Shared Key) you provide. This data is shared with the companion Home Screen widget through an iOS App Group container on the same device.
  • Stores appearance preferences locally on your device.

Accounts and data collection

  • It does not require you to create an account.
  • It does not operate a server that collects personal information.
  • The app sends pairing credentials directly to the TV you choose to control on your local network.

Third-party services

TV app icons

When you view the app list from a paired TV, RoomKey can load icons from addresses supplied by that TV. An address may point to an external server. If so, that server receives the network information needed to return the image, such as your public IP address, and may log the request under its own privacy practices.

Advertising and purchases

Ads and in-app purchases are disabled in this build.

Children

The App is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is disabled in the current version.

Data retention and deletion

To remove saved TVs, pairing credentials, and app preferences, use Settings → Reset All Settings in RoomKey or delete the app. You can revoke local network access at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network on your iPhone or iPad.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the RoomKey support page. The support tracker is public. Keep credentials and personal information out of public reports.
